## Dispositivo de pulso

Concept art for an original wearable device in Soulmonsters, a Brazilian 2D manga adventure. A square chunky wrist-worn device approx 55mm x 55mm, clearly larger than a watch yet credible for all trainers, one modular universal model. Main construction: matte deep petrol blue square housing with 4 bevel-cut corners like folded kite paper; frame warm brushed brass with flush rivets, ivory inset, one recessed luminous cyan diamond shaped like a kite with intersecting diagonals. Mechanically purposeful: at front, a ceramic guide slot projects ONE continuous thin cyan digital string with little light segments, and a robust foldover woven navy strap at the back. One large tactile copper side trigger operable even with gloves, no sprawling controls. Entire sheet one large 3/4 hero view at upper center, small clean side profile and view of the clasp underneath at bottom left, and lower-right demonstration of the exact same device strapped to a young trainer's wrist, palm open, projecting one unmistakable curved cyan filament directly to the little diamond-shaped chest knot of Rabbito (use first attached ref for Rabbito only, retain geometric paper ears, patch, cream & teal kite-paper body). In hero view show only a short filament sample. Each drawing spaced well apart on warm ivory blank paper, portrait 2:3, all shapes fully inside canvas; with dark outline, clean simple flat cel shading, matte fabric and brass, accessible toy-like anime product design, sports craft aesthetic, no neon cyberpunk, no glossy render, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no logo, no clock face, no HUD/card frame. A practical object that feels native to kite flying, warm handmade materials and a restrained single digital glow.
