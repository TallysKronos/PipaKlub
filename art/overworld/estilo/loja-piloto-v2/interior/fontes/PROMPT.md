## Prompt usado

Produce one transparent isolated cutaway pixel-art interior of the contemporary Brazilian kite and trading card hobby shop Linha & Papel, for a 2D adventure RPG. Match the FIRST supplied image as the exact 2:1 isometric camera and floor plan: four floor corners form a broad diamond, two rear walls visible, front walls removed except short skirting and the glass entrance in lower-left/front with tiny capacho; broad floor paths. Furniture stays in exactly the first image's arrangement: two long shelves against far wall, glazed cashier counter at central back, one low gondola display center-left, vertical kite showcase against left wall, two separated community craft/card tables with benches at right/front on dark muted teal floor rug. Make visible framed kites hung on rear wall, shelves neatly grouped rolls of paper, cards, a few spools and tools; actual miniature kites at built displays. No humans, text, HUD, logos, characters. Second reference is the EXTERIOR of the same store, borrow its color and construction: cream plaster, muted teal walls and navy/cyan window frame, coral lower trim, warm birch counters, side-window daylight. Show two big shop windows with clear perspective across the cream and teal walls, warm daylight from upper left, floors light beige with subdued diamond tile joints. Tiny selectively saturated kite yellow and coral, tech terminal at counter. Third reference is the studio's finished pixel art neighboring lab interior: borrow its tile density, 1 pixel edges, color clusters, transparent cutout, sharp cel shading, coherent shape cleanup, restrained detail. Stay clean and not noisy, no ambient gradient or fog, clean architectural beams, floor and objects clearly supported. Scene alone on true transparent background, generous margin around entire floor, full shop including corners. Strictly parallel 2:1 diagonal down 1 for 2 horizontally; no vanishing points or near-frontal view. Strongly recognizable as a warm modern Brazilian shop for kites and card-playing, with clear aisle.
