# Montanha — kit de prévia

Assets originais gerados em 30/10/2026 com image_gen.imagegen. Fontes completas preservadas em fontes/; cópias de execução em godot/assets/overworld/kits/montanha-v1. Nenhuma edição de imagem por IA após a geração. Manifesto com dimensões, âncoras e alfa junto dos assets de execução.

Direção: desenho 2D mangá/cartoon, contorno azul-escuro e sombreamento em planos; rocha cinza quente e musgo sálvia. Referência de linguagem gráfica Dofus/Wakfu, sem copiar mapas ou assets. A referência enviada pelo dono orientou caminhos elevados e becos.

## Solicitações de geração
- rocha.png: textura quadrada ortográfica de estratos largos de arenito cinza, fissuras grandes, musgo discreto, bordas repetíveis, desenho com contorno, dois tons, sem render 3D nem ruído fotográfico.
- crista.png: três espigões assimétricos unidos em uma única base, vista isométrica 2:1, sombra azul acinzentada, luz superior esquerda, fundo transparente e margem; âncora inferior 50%/94%.
- rochedos.png: um conjunto baixo e largo de seis rochas conectadas, formas arredondadas angulares, base irregular, vista isométrica 2:1, transparência, sem pedra flutuante nem sombra de chão.

As três fontes foram copiadas sem recorte, com alfa conferido por amostragem a cada quatro pixels. A crista e os rochedos possuem alfa zero; a superfície é opaca. A montagem usa escala uniforme, não estica sprites. Graminhas reutilizam o kit pantano-v1. Aprovação visual do dono pendente; assets não são animações.

## Revisão 2 — novos assets

### Prompt do panorama (especificação de geração)
Use case stylized-concept. Production background painting for an original 2D isometric manga adventure RPG mountain region, graphic language inspired by Dofus/Wakfu: clean dark blue ink contours, broad hard cel shaded shapes, no realism, no 3D render. Wide landscape 3:2. Elevated bird eye view over a continuous sprawling mountain landscape: massive warm slate-grey angular cliff ridges forming a horseshoe in the upper left and upper right, forested foothills at lower corners, many receding blue-green distant ridges in the upper horizon, patches of ochre dry grass and sage bushes on rock shelves. Strong coherent large mountain masses, not scattered boulders. Middle half of image is a broad muted grey-green mountainside sloping towards viewer, with little detail, intended to sit BEHIND live separately rendered game ledges. No foreground props covering center. Lower quarter has deep rocky valley, a thin winding stream far below, subtle cool depth haze. Warm upper-left sun, airy blue sky only in top 12 percent, clear layers of atmospheric depth. Irregular natural silhouettes and connected geology. No characters, buildings, UI, letters, text, roads, paths, bridges, grids or labels. Composition should convincingly ground a playable multi-level mountain placed over central mountainside. Original art.

### Prompt do maciço
Use case stylized-concept. A single large connected mountain BUTTRESS environment sprite, original 2D manga cartoon RPG, Dofus/Wakfu graphic language. A massive irregular cluster of three broad angular rocky shoulders, one tall back ridge and two lower front ledges, connected into ONE coherent foundation, NO separate floating boulders. Strong natural geological shape, wide roughly 1.3x as wide as tall. Rock is warm pale grey, hard navy-blue shadow planes, thick clean dark blue ink contour, broad cel shaded faces with large sweeping cracks and layered strata, a few sage-green moss cushions and ochre alpine grass on sheltered ledges. Strong bold silhouette, clean handdrawn game art, not realistic, no photo textures, no soft airbrush, no 3D render, no tiny details. Elevated 2:1 isometric view, upper-left sunlight. Fully visible asset with generous empty margins, broad irregular diamond ground footprint at bottom, bottom center anchor. Transparent background true empty alpha. No ground plane, no tile, no shadow cast on ground, no people, UI, text or lettering. Must look like part of a continuous mountain, not decorative pile of pebbles. Intended game display width450px, height350px.
