# Lacustrel — cavaleiro draconiano

## Definido pelo autor

Criar oito cavaleiros(as) draconianos com helmets próximos do princípio do Dukemon, placas com faces metalizadas de dragão e inspiração em cavaleiros/figuras guerreiras com lore. Reforço posterior: ousar em anatomia e silhueta, conforme duas imagens apresentadas.

## Proposta individual

Inspiração: Lancelot (Romance arturiano). Adulto, estágio IV.

Arma: Espada-linha de duas margens. Verbo: Conciliar.

Carrega dois juramentos incompatíveis e precisa escolher a quem proteger. Sua espada abre um corredor entre linhas cruzadas, mas não permite defender as duas margens sozinho.

## Construção e ação solicitadas

Create ONE original ADULT DRACONIAN KNIGHT SOULMONSTER, full-body three-quarter concept on plain WHITE with generous margins. Our attached OWN dragon knight is ONLY a finish and living-kite construction guide, NOT its identity or colors. Invent a completely distinct individual. 45% living expressive dragon-creature,45% actual functional living-kite anatomy,10% crisp dark selective stepped outline and CLEAN 2D MANGA. Adult imposing silhouette, small head relative to coherent torso, TWO arms and TWO legs, TWO clear hands, TWO separate grounded feet, ONE head. HELMET signature: upper shell sculpted as a DRAGON CRANIUM with a short snout-ridge over deep narrow eye slit, two swept small horn/ear fins, lower half CLOSED articulated KNIGHT VISOR with layered jaw plates; face concealed, NO animal head perched on human face, no exposed human face, no long animal muzzle, no baby eyes. Do not copy Gallantmon or any existing character's helmet/color layout. Armor has sculpted METALLIZED DRAGON FACES as clear integrated relief on shoulder, breast or knee plates: brows, tapered snout and closed jaws read as dragon masks; those are rigid plates NOT living secondary heads. Metal rendered with FLAT silver/colored cel facets and ONE deliberate hard highlight stripe, no 3D shine, gradients, photoreal metal or glass. Rich armor design with LARGE/MEDIUM/small hierarchy, legible joints, broad quiet plates between accents, proper thighs/hips/shoulder roots. Under armor body is broad taut colored PAPER membranes, few compression folds not muscle fibers/flesh or triangle tessellation. Bamboo inner edges and meaningful knot hinges; kite membranes form cloak or specialized armor sails rather than paper confetti. ONE rooted braided soul-rabiola with cord and terminal, distinct from cloak/weapon cords. No crystal automatically, no patchwork, orange eyes, all-over spikes, labels, panels, text, logos, scenery, extra characters or cropped equipment. Clean saturated 2D manga cel art with TWO HARD shadow tones, matte broad surfaces, sharp inked contour; not generic realistic fantasy armor. MALE KNIGHT, tall broadshouldered elegant lake duelist, long but robust mature torso and thighs, controlled sword stance. DEEP COBALT and silver metal armor with TURQUOISE paper underbody and lilac cape lining, small mint-green visor light. Helmet upper dragon skull has two lateral fin-like crests swept backward and short snout ridge, lower full CLOSED silver visor with dark central seam. TWO shoulder pauldrons are smooth METALLIC river-dragon faces elongated sideways, quiet broad silver chest with a blue V like two lake banks, no jewel. Holds ONE wide long straight translucent-appearing but FLAT ivory/cyan PAPER-LINE greatsword in BOTH hands, hands far apart on one grip, blade diagonal outward and uncropped; split bamboo framing and two thin tension cords explain blade, no second weapon. Waist has two overlapping broad kite coat tails, knees smooth, boots large. ONE blue-silver braided rabiola rooted rear pelvis with twin lilac pennants, distinct from coat. Adult oath-burdened calm, no gigantic cone lance or circular shield.

## Conferido na arte

Correção dirigida retirou a mão da lâmina. Uma mão segura a empunhadura e a outra está aberta; espada contínua com armação e tensão próprias. Faces prateadas nas ombreiras, visor fechado, duas pernas e uma rabiola. Ponta da espada próxima da borda direita, completa. Silhueta de duelista alta permanece uma das formas mais convencionais.

## Evidência versus invenção

[Lancelot](https://d.lib.rochester.edu/camelot/theme/lancelot.html) · University of Rochester · Camelot Project. Estudo acompanha mudanças literárias de Lancelot e seu serviço à rainha em Chrétien.

Aplicação: Conflito entre lealdades, duas margens e gesto de conciliação; espada de linha é invenção.

Nenhuma arte externa usada como input. O Tramadraco próprio forneceu somente acabamento e construção, sem vínculo evolutivo. Novos nomes, lore e vínculos com facções são propostas. Não há ligação automática com os onze cavaleiros, Armada dos Lobos, Crystais Pipas ou famílias anteriores. Pré-evoluções, obtenção, níveis e integração não definidos.
