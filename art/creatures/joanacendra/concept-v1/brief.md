# Joanacendra — cavaleiro draconiano

## Definido pelo autor

Criar oito cavaleiros(as) draconianos com helmets próximos do princípio do Dukemon, placas com faces metalizadas de dragão e inspiração em cavaleiros/figuras guerreiras com lore. Reforço posterior: ousar em anatomia e silhueta, conforme duas imagens apresentadas.

## Proposta individual

Inspiração: Joana d’Arc (Figura histórica; releitura ficcional). Adulto, estágio IV.

Arma: Estandarte de brasa. Verbo: Reunir.

Não escuta ordens do destino: reconhece as linhas de parceiros dispersos e ergue um estandarte para reuni-los. Sua força exige permanecer visível, expondo sua posição.

## Construção e ação solicitadas

Create ONE original ADULT DRACONIAN KNIGHT SOULMONSTER, full-body three-quarter concept on plain WHITE with generous margins. Our attached OWN dragon knight is ONLY a finish and living-kite construction guide, NOT its identity or colors. Invent a completely distinct individual. 45% living expressive dragon-creature,45% actual functional living-kite anatomy,10% crisp dark selective stepped outline and CLEAN 2D MANGA. Adult imposing silhouette, small head relative to coherent torso, TWO arms and TWO legs, TWO clear hands, TWO separate grounded feet, ONE head. HELMET signature: upper shell sculpted as a DRAGON CRANIUM with a short snout-ridge over deep narrow eye slit, two swept small horn/ear fins, lower half CLOSED articulated KNIGHT VISOR with layered jaw plates; face concealed, NO animal head perched on human face, no exposed human face, no long animal muzzle, no baby eyes. Do not copy Gallantmon or any existing character's helmet/color layout. Armor has sculpted METALLIZED DRAGON FACES as clear integrated relief on shoulder, breast or knee plates: brows, tapered snout and closed jaws read as dragon masks; those are rigid plates NOT living secondary heads. Metal rendered with FLAT silver/colored cel facets and ONE deliberate hard highlight stripe, no 3D shine, gradients, photoreal metal or glass. Rich armor design with LARGE/MEDIUM/small hierarchy, legible joints, broad quiet plates between accents, proper thighs/hips/shoulder roots. Under armor body is broad taut colored PAPER membranes, few compression folds not muscle fibers/flesh or triangle tessellation. Bamboo inner edges and meaningful knot hinges; kite membranes form cloak or specialized armor sails rather than paper confetti. ONE rooted braided soul-rabiola with cord and terminal, distinct from cloak/weapon cords. No crystal automatically, no patchwork, orange eyes, all-over spikes, labels, panels, text, logos, scenery, extra characters or cropped equipment. Clean saturated 2D manga cel art with TWO HARD shadow tones, matte broad surfaces, sharp inked contour; not generic realistic fantasy armor. FEMALE adult dragon banner KNIGHT, strong compact torso and sturdy legs, severe grounded commander shape, full practical armor no bare skin. PEARL WHITE/silver metal with smoky CHARCOAL broad chest plates, vivid MAGENTA paper underfolds and warm brass joints, small teal eye slit. Upper dragon-skull helmet broad low brow and very small two rear swept horns; closed silver lattice-jaw visor, no open face or hair. Broad breastplate is ONE embossed METALLIC dragon face centered on sternum, shoulder guards relatively modest so chest is focal; articulated waist and large knees. Carries ONE tall continuous bamboo bannerstaff in right hand, massive asymmetrical TAUT paper flag in white/magenta with ONE original flame-knot graphic no text or real heraldry, uncropped; left hand rests open on chest, not another weapon. Short charred-edge dark cape has only three big clean torn shapes, not noisy shredded cloth. ONE braided magenta soul-rabiola rooted back waist ends in small ivory flame fan, separate from flag. Stance rallying forward, adult solemn authoritative, small helmet over robust chest.

## Conferido na arte

Dois braços e duas pernas; mão na haste, outra sobre o peito; face prateada no peitoral e nas joelheiras. Correção encurtou o remate dourado para deixar margem superior. Rabiola distinta da capa e estandarte. Corpo robusto com visor fechado e cor magenta nas membranas.

## Evidência versus invenção

[Joan of Arc · Jules Bastien-Lepage](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/435621) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art. Texto curatorial situa Joana na Lorena medieval e comenta pose/expressão na interpretação posterior de seu chamado.

Aplicação: Coragem de reunir uma formação; estandarte, brasa e função da criatura são invenção, não alegação histórica ou teológica.

Nenhuma arte externa usada como input. O Tramadraco próprio forneceu somente acabamento e construção, sem vínculo evolutivo. Novos nomes, lore e vínculos com facções são propostas. Não há ligação automática com os onze cavaleiros, Armada dos Lobos, Crystais Pipas ou famílias anteriores. Pré-evoluções, obtenção, níveis e integração não definidos.
