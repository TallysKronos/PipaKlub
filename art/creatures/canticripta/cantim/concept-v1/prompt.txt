A rounded indigo seed body whose front ivory fold is the magenta-eyed gentle face. Two short bamboo antennae and six tiny tucked tab-feet, three on each side. Clean 2D manga ink, saturated indigo/ivory/jade with flat hard cel shading, taut paper surfaces. Full little creature on white, wide margin, no text.
