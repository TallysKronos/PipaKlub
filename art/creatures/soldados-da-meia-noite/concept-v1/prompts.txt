Severe nocturnal presence, very different silhouette from conventional Dracula cosplay. Three-quarter pose readable and not obscured by studies. Vespergume, commander, vampire bat species. Tall narrow upright silhouette with giant angular ear blades, leaf nose, fang overbite, long digitigrade legs and one asymmetrically extended shoulder sail. One arm lifted like a silent conductor, other low. The shoulder sail is a folded ivory kite membrane stretched between three clearly articulated bone spars grown from scapula and slotted into elbow, becoming a diagonal crest, one thin long split ribbon tail counterweight. Deep plum, ivory, garnet red, tiny brass on the joint. Narrow blood-red eyes. Noble cruel composure. No ordinary cape or full wings.
