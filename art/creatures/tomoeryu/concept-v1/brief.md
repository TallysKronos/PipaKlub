# Tomoeryu — cavaleiro draconiano

## Definido pelo autor

Criar oito cavaleiros(as) draconianos com helmets próximos do princípio do Dukemon, placas com faces metalizadas de dragão e inspiração em cavaleiros/figuras guerreiras com lore. Reforço posterior: ousar em anatomia e silhueta, conforme duas imagens apresentadas.

## Proposta individual

Inspiração: Tomoe Gozen (Figura semihistórica da tradição guerreira japonesa). Adulto, estágio IV.

Arma: Naginata de corrente. Verbo: Desviar.

Defende a retaguarda com curvas de linha em vez de golpes frontais. A lâmina desvia uma carga por vez; tentar deter todas rompe sua postura.

## Construção e ação solicitadas

Create ONE original ADULT DRACONIAN KNIGHT SOULMONSTER, full-body three-quarter concept on plain WHITE with generous margins. Our attached OWN dragon knight is ONLY a finish and living-kite construction guide, NOT its identity or colors. Invent a completely distinct individual. 45% living expressive dragon-creature,45% actual functional living-kite anatomy,10% crisp dark selective stepped outline and CLEAN 2D MANGA. Adult imposing silhouette, small head relative to coherent torso, TWO arms and TWO legs, TWO clear hands, TWO separate grounded feet, ONE head. HELMET signature: upper shell sculpted as a DRAGON CRANIUM with a short snout-ridge over deep narrow eye slit, two swept small horn/ear fins, lower half CLOSED articulated KNIGHT VISOR with layered jaw plates; face concealed, NO animal head perched on human face, no exposed human face, no long animal muzzle, no baby eyes. Do not copy Gallantmon or any existing character's helmet/color layout. Armor has sculpted METALLIZED DRAGON FACES as clear integrated relief on shoulder, breast or knee plates: brows, tapered snout and closed jaws read as dragon masks; those are rigid plates NOT living secondary heads. Metal rendered with FLAT silver/colored cel facets and ONE deliberate hard highlight stripe, no 3D shine, gradients, photoreal metal or glass. Rich armor design with LARGE/MEDIUM/small hierarchy, legible joints, broad quiet plates between accents, proper thighs/hips/shoulder roots. Under armor body is broad taut colored PAPER membranes, few compression folds not muscle fibers/flesh or triangle tessellation. Bamboo inner edges and meaningful knot hinges; kite membranes form cloak or specialized armor sails rather than paper confetti. ONE rooted braided soul-rabiola with cord and terminal, distinct from cloak/weapon cords. No crystal automatically, no patchwork, orange eyes, all-over spikes, labels, panels, text, logos, scenery, extra characters or cropped equipment. Clean saturated 2D manga cel art with TWO HARD shadow tones, matte broad surfaces, sharp inked contour; not generic realistic fantasy armor. FEMALE adult draconic NAGINATA knight, sturdy athletic hips and strong legs, elegant compact upper body, full armor no skin. INK NAVY and vivid CORAL-red armor with ivory living-paper membranes, jade-green small visor slit and brass knot rivets. Helmet combines short DRAGON SKULL upper crest with TWO broad antler-like bamboo side arcs (ONLY two smooth arcs, not branch forest), full closed layered SILVER lower knight visor no human face, no samurai moustache. Broad right shoulder laminated guard sculpted as a metallic long river-dragon FACE; layered skirt made of FIVE broad paper-and-lacquer plates, left shoulder lighter and ONE dragonface knee. Holds ONE long continuous naginata in BOTH hands, curved blade ivory/silver with red edge and ONE small navy tension sail at root, all fingers/grip coherent, no second staff or sword. Short angular two-panel coral back sash not wings. ONE thin navy braided soul-rabiola rooted back waist with forked coral terminal distinct from weapon ties. Lateral pivot defensive pose on TWO firmly planted boots; look toward blade, mature disciplined authority.

## Conferido na arte

Dois braços e duas pernas, duas mãos agarram a mesma haste contínua; ponta e base completas. Dois arcos largos no helmet, viseira fechada, uma grande ombreira dracônica e joelho em relevo. Cinco painéis de saia e duas abas posteriores. Rabiola única. Silhueta de placas segmentadas, postura menos dinâmica que o giro imaginado no brief.

## Evidência versus invenção

[Tomoe Gozen](https://samuraimuseum.de/en/learn/tomoe-gozen/) · Samurai Museum Berlin. Apresenta tradição da guerreira japonesa Tomoe Gozen. Historicidade não deve ser equiparada a documentação integral dos episódios épicos.

Aplicação: Giro, defesa de retaguarda e leitura de placas segmentadas; não afirmar ordem europeia de cavalaria.

Nenhuma arte externa usada como input. O Tramadraco próprio forneceu somente acabamento e construção, sem vínculo evolutivo. Novos nomes, lore e vínculos com facções são propostas. Não há ligação automática com os onze cavaleiros, Armada dos Lobos, Crystais Pipas ou famílias anteriores. Pré-evoluções, obtenção, níveis e integração não definidos.
