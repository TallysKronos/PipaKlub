Create ONE original evolved Soulmonsters AZULTOR concept using the attached EXISTING AZULAO as the primary visual and anatomical reference. This is a corrective design test responding to the creator: "45% creature, 45% kite, 10% pixel / 2D manga." These are art-direction weights, not a measured percentage chart. The reference is the project's own approved creature. Evolution must visibly continue THIS living kite construction, not turn it into an ordinary feathered hippogriff.
ONE full-body 3/4 creature on a genuinely TRANSPARENT background, generous margins, no text, no action panels, no environment, no black rectangle, no shadow oval.
BODY PLAN: compact guardian hippogriff suggestion with FOUR weight-bearing limbs and TWO separate shoulder wings, ONE head and ONE complete ribbon tail. Avian front, powerful short equine rear. But the creature is BUILT as a living kite, not biological flesh with bamboo glued on. Do NOT draw fluffy fur, a realistic bird head or a normal horse barrel.
HEAD: an expressive three-quarter folded diamond-kite face with a clear golden bamboo diamond perimeter and tied blue corner knots, scaled like a head above the front chest, NOT a huge flat shield pasted over the whole creature. Its folded central crease creates a proud wedge-shaped brow and an integrated golden paper beak that hooks down. Angular anime ink eyes are alive on the ivory face plane, focused and stubborn. Short pair of pointed cobalt paper crest vanes tied at the upper vertex, evolved from the two small kite tabs of Azulão. The head is three-dimensional shallow folded paper, not a round eagle skull. Compact cream folded collar, no natural feather mane.
TORSO: a substantial air-filled folded paper kite-box chest, tapering to a short raised paper haunch. Cobalt and ultramarine main panels, warm ivory chest chevron, restrained turquoise underside. Broad paper planes that flex and overlap into the shoulders and rump, with selected golden living spars actually supporting seams at the sides. Enough volume and believable weight to feel like a creature, but kite construction changes the silhouette of the torso itself. No scattered armor plates, no ornate mechanical robot, no all-over triangles or stitched patch wallpaper.
LIMBS: FOUR coherent articulated supports. Forelegs are strong folded ivory-and-blue paper sleeves over short flexible golden spar cores; three hooked golden folded talons per front foot spread to anchor against a gust. Hindlimbs are compact equine folded-paper haunches leading to short jointed supports and charcoal-blue trapezoid folded hooves. Blue knot joints belong inside the anatomy at two or three important flexion points. Paper has living flexibility, no open hollow tube sockets, no exposed ball-and-socket robot parts. Clearly separate forelegs, hindlegs, wing roots. All four feet shown supported, not floating.
WINGS: each is a broad birdlike folded KITE FAN of THREE large connected vanes, not a bat membrane. Feather-like cut silhouette made of paper panels edged and tensioned by a continuous bent bamboo spar and just a few knots. The familiar ivory cloud motif becomes ONE big simple flowing field across the fan, not repeated curls everywhere. Wing cups curve forward around the shoulder like an anchor shelter, poised to receive wind and spring it back. Slight asymmetry of the two wing angles shows a thinking, alert creature. Pair of wings attach above the front legs, not instead of them.
RABIOLA: two genuine thin ribbon lengths with tied joints lead from the rump to a compact split cloud-kite terminal with a small ivory paper face-like curl shape, closely related to the reference's tail. It is a flexible line-and-paper structure with airy negative space, not a fluffy white mammal tail. Entire tail within image.
FINISH: match the reference's crisp dark stepped contour and deliberate angular color boundaries, with large clean flat 2D manga color areas. Selective small pixel-like stair steps at outer outline and corners, not jagged noise over every surface. Rich cobalt, ultramarine, cream, golden bamboo, tiny coral knot accents only. One or two clear cel shadow tones, small paper-fold highlights. No gradients, no painterly texture, no realistic lighting, no 3D render, no smooth plastic, no generic soft mascot. The face must remain warm, expressive and alive, not an ornamental faceless paper toy. Do not copy any external franchise design.
The main test is: remove the wings mentally and the head, chest, feet, joints and tail STILL unmistakably belong to a living KITE creature. Distinctive compact rooted stance, head slightly turned as if noticing a change in wind. Creature personality and anatomy + kite construction are equally dominant.
