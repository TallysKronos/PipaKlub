# Coroabissal — Quatro Marés

## Definido pelo autor

Criar quatro Soulmonsters aquáticos do grupo “Quatro Marés”. Direção vigente: 45% criatura, 45% pipa viva, 10% contorno e 2D mangá. A alma permanece na rabiola.

## Proposta de identidade

Adulto · IV Pandorga. Preamar: sustentar e amortecer. Recebe a pressão da água e protege as passagens durante a cheia.

Paleta: azul-noite, cobalto, vinho e marfim; olhos verde-menta.

ADULT Coroabissal, high-tide guardian: monumental compact WHALE-like original living kite beast, deep broad chest and blunt proud sperm-whale-like head, no trunk, no horns, no dragon features, mature narrow MINT eyes recessed under a stepped ivory brow. Distinct broad whale jaw with short toothlike PAPER folds only a few at corner. TWO powerful flipper fins, a compact body narrowing to TWO horizontal caudal sail lobes (not fish vertical tail), NO legs. Rich midnight/cobalt paper body, ivory throat, WINE red side planes. Grand dorsal crest is a spiraling vaulted paper-and-bamboo PRESSURE CHAMBER shaped like half a nautilus arch growing organically from back, not literal ship, robot or cannon. Mature substantial overlapping navy/copper armor at head crown and shoulder-flipper roots, rib-supported broad smooth inflatable paper flank volumes, few meaningful red knots at load points. A flat braided tide-knot seal under jaw, NO crystal. ONE separate complete soul-rabiola rooted at upper rear body before caudal fin, thin braided red/navy line with small folded shell terminal. Pose swimming forward three-quarter, both flippers and horizontal tail readable, imposing well-constructed mass, no tiny scale/fur texture.

## Observado e conferido

Duas nadadeiras anteriores e cauda em dois lobos horizontais, corpo compacto sem patas. Câmara dorsal em espiral, boca e olho verde-menta maduros. Rabiola fina no dorso posterior separada da cauda; nó frontal tem abas curtas decorativas, sem segunda linha longa.

Limites: concept de fundo branco, sem grid de pixel art técnico, recorte, vistas extras, rig ou animação certificados.
