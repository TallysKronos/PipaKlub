# Reciférreo — Quatro Marés

## Definido pelo autor

Criar quatro Soulmonsters aquáticos do grupo “Quatro Marés”. Direção vigente: 45% criatura, 45% pipa viva, 10% contorno e 2D mangá. A alma permanece na rabiola.

## Proposta de identidade

Adulto · IV Pandorga. Baixamar: ancorar e resguardar. Mantém os abrigos entre recifes quando a água recua.

Paleta: verde-celadon, carvão, açafrão e marfim; olhos rosa-gelo.

ADULT Reciferreo, low-tide guardian: massive LOW original CRUSTACEAN living kite bastion, not humanoid knight or turtle. EXACTLY SIX stout walking legs (THREE each side), plus TWO distinct giant front pincer arms, eight functional limbs total; six feet clearly spaced, no hidden extra foot. Wide flattened celadon/charcoal armored PAPER carapace in THREE descending offset terraces resembling exposed tidal shelves, broad folded membrane joins, ivory low belly; saffron warm accent edges. Head a SMALL mature angular crustacean mask set into front, TWO short eye-stalks with narrow icy PINK eyes, tucked jaw plates; no human face or gigantic cartoon eyes. LEFT claw is a broad shallow fan-shaped shielding shovel, RIGHT claw a compact deep anchoring hook, recognizable two-part pincers with hinge roots, not weapons held in hands. Substantial matte charcoal/copper forearm and leg-root armor; broad paper fin-like kelp vanes in dark celadon behind shoulder terraces, only FOUR well-designed large ones, not realistic plants growing everywhere. Strong bamboo perimeter ribs and saffron functional knots hold shell load, no strings binding creature like mummy. One braided tide-knot emblem on front keel, NOT crystal. ONE complete braided rear soul-rabiola ending a stepped tidal-notch paper flag. Powerful grounded defensive stance three-quarter, entire six legs/two claws readable. No natural rough crustacean texture, no rocks/scenery hiding limb roots.

Correção dirigida retirou uma pata excedente atrás da pinça-escudo. Seis patas de apoio e dois braços de pinça. Olhos rosa-gelo em pedúnculos, placas em três patamares, rabiola única. Três vanes dorsais grandes estão visíveis; a quarta pedida não foi certificada nesta vista.

Limites: concept de fundo branco, sem grid de pixel art técnico, recorte, vistas extras, rig ou animação certificados.
