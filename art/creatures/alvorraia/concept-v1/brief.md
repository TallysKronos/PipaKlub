# Alvorraia — Quatro Marés

## Definido pelo autor

Criar quatro Soulmonsters aquáticos do grupo “Quatro Marés”. Direção vigente: 45% criatura, 45% pipa viva, 10% contorno e 2D mangá. A alma permanece na rabiola.

## Proposta de identidade

Adulto · IV Pandorga. Enchente: erguer e conduzir. Abre caminho quando a água avança, elevando parceiros sobre obstáculos.

Paleta: turquesa, coral, marfim e índigo; olhos violeta.

ADULT Alvorraia, incoming-tide guardian: an immense original manta-like living kite creature, NO legs, TWO broad swept pectoral sail fins rising in a powerful asymmetric lifting gesture, integrated short forward cephalic lobes made from TWO curled bamboo-and-paper guide vanes, not ears/horns. Small mature angular ray face on front body fold with narrow violet eyes, flat folded jaw and subtle gill-like paper slots, not bird beak/dragon head. Broad central diamond-bellied body made of ivory tensioned paper with coral and turquoise currents painted in big graphical forms; indigo lower sail plane. Bamboo leading spars grow from actual fin roots, coral-edged layered shoulder armor, matte copper sparse corner clamps, functional tide-knot at keel (a BRAIDED KNOT, NOT A GEM). Fins are several huge structured cut-paper vanes, not natural leathery skin or bird feathers. ONE long slim braided rear soul-rabiola, replacing ordinary sting tail, ends a coral/ivory fan flag, full root visible. Surface-powerful adult presence, compact body, long wide wings with clear left/right origins. Few connected splash-like PAPER edge shapes, no actual water effects hiding anatomy.

## Observado e conferido

Duas nadadeiras-velas e dois lobos cefálicos curvos, sem patas. Rosto adulto estreito, fendas inferiores e rabiola única com terminal de leque. Varetas e nós sustentam o disco corporal; contorno completo apesar de margens mais estreitas que o pedido.

Limites: concept de fundo branco, sem grid de pixel art técnico, recorte, vistas extras, rig ou animação certificados.
