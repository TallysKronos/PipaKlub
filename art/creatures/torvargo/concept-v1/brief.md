# Torvargo — Lorde do Primeiro Impacto

## Definido pelo autor

Criar oito Lords Creatures com base no estudo destrinchado da fera armadurada.

Direção: 45% criatura, 45% pipa viva e 10% acabamento pixel/2D mangá; corpo e rosto bestiais, proteção localizada, cor por material.

## Proposta

Adulto (IV), Javali-aríete. Ação: **Romper**.

Abre um caminho pela barreira, mas precisa reconhecer o que há atrás dela antes de avançar. A marca de um golpe certo é a passagem que permanece de pé.

## Construção solicitada

Create ONE NEW ORIGINAL adult SOULMONSTER LORD CREATURE, not an ordinary human knight with animal head, not an animal simply dressed in armor. Our attached OWN griffin is only a guide to clean 2D manga finish and broad living-paper anatomy; never copy its species, blue palette, diamond crystal, silhouette or wings. Design from a specific BEAST BODY and physical action first, add LOCALIZED functional armor second. 45% expressive living creature,45% functional living kite construction,10% selective stepped dark contour and CLEAN FLAT 2D MANGA. FACE exposed mature monstrous nonhuman anatomy, small narrow eyes with iris/pupil, characteristic skull/jaw/muzzle, no human face, smiling puppy, giant eyes, closed helmet or robotic metal mask. Living body is broad taut colored PAPER membrane masses with 2-3 meaningful compression folds and a FEW directional folded-paper tufts at cheek/neck/joints; not realistic flesh/fur/scales/muscle fibers, not a solid origami toy with triangular tessellation. Paper supports/ribs/knots appear ONLY where they carry load and don't outline every surface. Strong adult proportion: head smaller than major body masses, hands/feet/neck designed for action, root anatomy coherent. Considerable visible creature torso and limbs between protective pieces, not full suit of armor. Armor LOCALIZED in 2-3 regions with broad quiet surfaces, thickness and clear joints. Few large details, medium construction, small restrained signature. COLOR by material: dominant BODY neutral or one colored paper mass, ONE ARMOR color in continuous fields, ONE CAPE/SAIL contrast if needed, ONE tiny energy/eye accent. No multicolor borders/inlays in every plate, rainbow bands, ornate filigree, repeated gold trim or face relief on every plate. One base + one hard shadow + restrained hard highlight per material, matte, no gradients/gloss/PBR/noise. ONE complete rooted soul-rabiola with cord and terminal; extra anatomical tails/wing supports never become extra souls. No automatic crystal, orange eyes, text, labels, panel grid, copied insignia, external franchise motifs, scenery or other creatures. FULL creature three-quarter action-ready, all limbs/equipment/tail complete on plain WHITE with wide margins. Each signature structure must participate in its physical action, not decorative halo. BIPED boar-RHINO hybrid BEAST lord with huge deep forward-leaning torso and humped shoulder ridge, small low short square BOAR skull with flat angular DARK nose, small MINT eyes, TWO short thick ivory folded tusks curving SIDEWAYS not up into eyes, thick slate cheek paper tufts. TWO powerful LOW arms ending THREE large clawed digits plus thumb and TWO compact weighty legs with split hooved-claw feet. BODY dominant CHARCOAL taut paper, broad quiet ivory belly/throat tension membrane. ARMOR color dull CORAL RED, localized on ONE huge asymmetric near shoulder IMPACT SHELL and BOTH squat forearm bracers; chest/upperarms/thighs visible and not muscular human abs. Signature shoulder shell has broad closed crescent battering face with one inset bamboo load arch, no snarling heads or spikes; near arm angles forward protecting head, far arm counterbalances fully visible. TWO narrow dark-red rear paper pennant plates stabilize rump, no broad cape. ONE charcoal braided rabiola rooted rear pelvis with single coral blunt-chevron flag. Feet wide in ready-to-charge pose, not cartoon pig, no helmet or giant hammer. Coral only armor fields, no colored trims.

## Observado

Dois braços, duas pernas curtas, face de javali com presas laterais e ombro de impacto assimétrico. Corpo carvão/marfim exposto entre as placas coral. Rabiola única completa. A mão distante tem dedos parcialmente sobrepostos; vistas técnicas futuras devem resolver a contagem e o apoio.

## Desconhecido

Nomes, títulos e lore são propostas. Pré-evoluções, facção, obtenção, poderes, escala canônica e integração não definidos. Concepts não certificam recortes, rig, animações ou grade técnica de pixel art.
