# Najarcal — Lorde do Círculo Aberto

## Definido pelo autor

Criar oito Lords Creatures com base no estudo destrinchado da fera armadurada.

Direção: 45% criatura, 45% pipa viva e 10% acabamento pixel/2D mangá; corpo e rosto bestiais, proteção localizada, cor por material.

## Proposta

Adulto (IV), Naja de campânula. Ação: **Conter**.

Prende a ameaça num círculo que sempre conserva uma saída. Se fechar a última abertura, aprisiona também quem veio salvar.

## Construção solicitada

Create ONE NEW ORIGINAL adult SOULMONSTER LORD CREATURE, not an ordinary human knight with animal head, not an animal simply dressed in armor. Our attached OWN griffin is only a guide to clean 2D manga finish and broad living-paper anatomy; never copy its species, blue palette, diamond crystal, silhouette or wings. Design from a specific BEAST BODY and physical action first, add LOCALIZED functional armor second. 45% expressive living creature,45% functional living kite construction,10% selective stepped dark contour and CLEAN FLAT 2D MANGA. FACE exposed mature monstrous nonhuman anatomy, small narrow eyes with iris/pupil, characteristic skull/jaw/muzzle, no human face, smiling puppy, giant eyes, closed helmet or robotic metal mask. Living body is broad taut colored PAPER membrane masses with 2-3 meaningful compression folds and a FEW directional folded-paper tufts at cheek/neck/joints; not realistic flesh/fur/scales/muscle fibers, not a solid origami toy with triangular tessellation. Paper supports/ribs/knots appear ONLY where they carry load and don't outline every surface. Strong adult proportion: head smaller than major body masses, hands/feet/neck designed for action, root anatomy coherent. Considerable visible creature torso and limbs between protective pieces, not full suit of armor. Armor LOCALIZED in 2-3 regions with broad quiet surfaces, thickness and clear joints. Few large details, medium construction, small restrained signature. COLOR by material: dominant BODY neutral or one colored paper mass, ONE ARMOR color in continuous fields, ONE CAPE/SAIL contrast if needed, ONE tiny energy/eye accent. No multicolor borders/inlays in every plate, rainbow bands, ornate filigree, repeated gold trim or face relief on every plate. One base + one hard shadow + restrained hard highlight per material, matte, no gradients/gloss/PBR/noise. ONE complete rooted soul-rabiola with cord and terminal; extra anatomical tails/wing supports never become extra souls. No automatic crystal, orange eyes, text, labels, panel grid, copied insignia, external franchise motifs, scenery or other creatures. FULL creature three-quarter action-ready, all limbs/equipment/tail complete on plain WHITE with wide margins. Each signature structure must participate in its physical action, not decorative halo. SERPENT LORD with TWO long articulated NONHUMAN arms and NO legs, ONE very thick grounded S-coil tail supporting high powerful upper trunk, no human waist or abs. Small mature COBRA paper head, broad LOW upper skull, long shallow flattened snout, THREE short ivory teeth, small deep BLUE eyes under paper brow, no human smile, crown or animal helmet. BODY dominant pale SAND/ivory taut paper with only ONE broad dark gray throatfold; hood has THREE huge paper panels on curved bamboo spars, broad and calm, not manytiny scales. ARMOR one deep TEAL color confined to paired upperarm load cuffs and TWO large external tail-anchor plates, big exposed torso and coil. Signature arms each carry ONE open C-shaped rigid TEAL restraint hoop attached at wrist by a knot hinge; not weapons held floating, open palms still visible, hoop geometry leaves an obvious opening. Twoarms spread at different levels to shape a containment circle, body upright yet serpentine and alive. ONE thin dark teal braided soul-rabiola at tip of anatomical tail with ivory single crescent flag, no extra cord. No cape/halo/crystals/runes, matte flat large masses and clear negative space in hoops.

## Observado

Dois braços e nenhuma perna, sustentação por cauda serpentina contínua. Dois aros abertos conectados aos punhos, duas palmas expostas e capuz de varetas. Uma rabiola ao final da cauda. A gola ganhou dobras que lembram uma juba curta de papel; é construção fantástica, não estudo zoológico.

## Desconhecido

Nomes, títulos e lore são propostas. Pré-evoluções, facção, obtenção, poderes, escala canônica e integração não definidos. Concepts não certificam recortes, rig, animações ou grade técnica de pixel art.
