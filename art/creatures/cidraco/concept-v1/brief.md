# Cidraco — cavaleiro draconiano

## Definido pelo autor

Criar oito cavaleiros(as) draconianos com helmets próximos do princípio do Dukemon, placas com faces metalizadas de dragão e inspiração em cavaleiros/figuras guerreiras com lore. Reforço posterior: ousar em anatomia e silhueta, conforme duas imagens apresentadas.

## Proposta individual

Inspiração: El Cid (Figura histórica e tradição épica). Adulto, estágio IV.

Arma: Sabre e escudo de passagem. Verbo: Escoltar.

A fama o precede, mas ele protege viajantes que não reconhecem seu brasão. Aprende a abandonar a vitória espetacular para manter uma travessia segura.

## Construção e ação solicitadas

Create ONE original ADULT DRACONIAN KNIGHT SOULMONSTER, full-body three-quarter concept on plain WHITE with generous margins. Our attached OWN dragon knight is ONLY a finish and living-kite construction guide, NOT its identity or colors. Invent a completely distinct individual. 45% living expressive dragon-creature,45% actual functional living-kite anatomy,10% crisp dark selective stepped outline and CLEAN 2D MANGA. Adult imposing silhouette, small head relative to coherent torso, TWO arms and TWO legs, TWO clear hands, TWO separate grounded feet, ONE head. HELMET signature: upper shell sculpted as a DRAGON CRANIUM with a short snout-ridge over deep narrow eye slit, two swept small horn/ear fins, lower half CLOSED articulated KNIGHT VISOR with layered jaw plates; face concealed, NO animal head perched on human face, no exposed human face, no long animal muzzle, no baby eyes. Do not copy Gallantmon or any existing character's helmet/color layout. Armor has sculpted METALLIZED DRAGON FACES as clear integrated relief on shoulder, breast or knee plates: brows, tapered snout and closed jaws read as dragon masks; those are rigid plates NOT living secondary heads. Metal rendered with FLAT silver/colored cel facets and ONE deliberate hard highlight stripe, no 3D shine, gradients, photoreal metal or glass. Rich armor design with LARGE/MEDIUM/small hierarchy, legible joints, broad quiet plates between accents, proper thighs/hips/shoulder roots. Under armor body is broad taut colored PAPER membranes, few compression folds not muscle fibers/flesh or triangle tessellation. Bamboo inner edges and meaningful knot hinges; kite membranes form cloak or specialized armor sails rather than paper confetti. ONE rooted braided soul-rabiola with cord and terminal, distinct from cloak/weapon cords. No crystal automatically, no patchwork, orange eyes, all-over spikes, labels, panels, text, logos, scenery, extra characters or cropped equipment. Clean saturated 2D manga cel art with TWO HARD shadow tones, matte broad surfaces, sharp inked contour; not generic realistic fantasy armor. MALE adult DRAGON KNIGHT, heavily built older veteran tank with square chest, thick arms and short powerful legs, not slim. BURNT SAFFRON, rich OXBLOOD and aged silver armor, charcoal living-paper underbody and emerald tiny visor eyes, no orange eyes. Closed helmet has broad low draconic skull brow and short curling side horns framing knight cheek/visor, full lower layered silver mask, mature stern. LEFT shoulder is one massive sculpted metallic dragon HEAD-face pauldron in silver/oxblood, RIGHT shoulder smaller, BOTH knees have blunt miniature dragonface relief, chest broad quiet saffron planes. One curved SINGLE-EDGE fantasy sabre held outward in right hand and small HEXAGONAL kite buckler in left with paper star-fold geometry (no religious symbols), no extra weapons. Short thick oxblood cape flows behind one leg, decorative bamboo edge ribs and anchored knots, no fur beard. ONE heavy ochre/saffron braided rabiola rooted rear waist ends oxblood caravan pennant. Grounded escort stance, all hands feet visible, not mounted or centaur.

## Conferido na arte

Dois braços e duas pernas, sabre e broquel em mãos distintas. Ombreira assimétrica e grande, joelheiras em relevo, peito amplo quieto. Helmet fechado, olhos verdes, rabiola única distinta da capa. Diferença de proporções existe, mas a anatomia ainda é a do cavaleiro bípede convencional.

## Evidência versus invenção

Aplicação: Veterano cuja fama não resolve o dever de escolta; não copiar conflitos religiosos para a campanha.

Nenhuma arte externa usada como input. O Tramadraco próprio forneceu somente acabamento e construção, sem vínculo evolutivo. Novos nomes, lore e vínculos com facções são propostas. Não há ligação automática com os onze cavaleiros, Armada dos Lobos, Crystais Pipas ou famílias anteriores. Pré-evoluções, obtenção, níveis e integração não definidos.
