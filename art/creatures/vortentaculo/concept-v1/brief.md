# Vortentáculo — Quatro Marés

## Definido pelo autor

Criar quatro Soulmonsters aquáticos do grupo “Quatro Marés”. Direção vigente: 45% criatura, 45% pipa viva, 10% contorno e 2D mangá. A alma permanece na rabiola.

## Proposta de identidade

Adulto · IV Pandorga. Vazante: recolher e desembaraçar. Libera o fluxo retido e devolve às margens o que a corrente arrastou.

Paleta: ameixa, violeta profundo, ocre e marfim; olhos azul-gelo.

ADULT Vortentaculo, ebb-tide guardian: original mature OCTOPUS living kite creature with EXACTLY EIGHT rooted flexible paper arms around a compact central underside, no human hands, legs or extra tentacles. Show FOUR broad foreground arms spreading in readable different S-curves and FOUR rear arms shorter or raised, all roots separate and traceable; each arm tapers to ONE tip. Tall angular plum/violet folded mantle like a pleated tidal funnel, small focused ice-blue cephalopod eyes on lower mantle, small tucked central beak underside mostly concealed, NOT human or bird face. OCHRE and ivory broad underside paper panels; large flat knot-discs along underside of arms act like suction anchors, few visible ones not hundreds tiny circles. Functional bamboo short segmented ribs at arm bases and important bends, matte ochre/copper armored cuffs on FOUR major arms, remaining four lighter. Upper mantle has broad stepped crown of THREE large layered directional sail plates, not wizard hat. One forward side siphon is a short CURVED FOLDED spout on body, not mechanical tube. ONE distinguishable thin braided soul-rabiola attached to BACK of mantle near top, with a purple/ivory crescent pennant; don't confuse with eight anatomical arms. Calm cunning mature command, spreading/draining gesture, no props, no crystal, no mottled realistic octopus skin.

## Observado e conferido

Oito braços com pontas distintas: quatro à frente e quatro atrás. Sifão lateral curto é parte do corpo, rabiola fina sai do topo posterior do manto. Olhos azul-gelo e nós-discos nas faces inferiores. Raízes legíveis nesta pose, conferir verso antes de rig.

Limites: concept de fundo branco, sem grid de pixel art técnico, recorte, vistas extras, rig ou animação certificados.
